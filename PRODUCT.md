# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Ahmad Kurniawan's portfolio speaks first to recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients who need to understand the person behind the work before judging fit. They arrive with limited time, looking for credibility, range, and a clear signal that Ahmad can move from interface craft to full product execution.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio exists to introduce Ahmad as a developer worth remembering, then give visitors enough proof to continue into projects, GitHub activity, and contact paths. Success means visitors leave with a coherent mental picture of Ahmad, not just a list of technologies.

## Positioning

Ahmad is a full-stack builder who can turn ideas into polished, working digital products.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: The primary outcome is for visitors to know Ahmad well enough to continue exploring; contact and project links act as follow-through paths.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Ahmad builds complete digital products, with both technical range and visual care.
- Belief ladder: Ahmad is real and credible; Ahmad has breadth beyond a single stack; Ahmad can ship polished experiences; Ahmad is worth contacting or shortlisting.
- Proof on hand: Live GitHub data, featured repositories, experience timeline, technology stack, and contact links already exist in the project.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, and capable. The interface should feel composed and trustworthy, with enough polish to signal taste but not so much spectacle that it distracts from Ahmad's story.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio tropes: repetitive card grids, decorative gradient text, effects that exist only to look impressive, and template-like section scaffolding. Also avoid extremes: too busy, too corporate, too casual, or too visually loud for recruiter and client contexts.

## Design Principles

- Lead with person before proof: make Ahmad memorable before asking visitors to inspect artifacts.
- Show full-stack range without turning the page into a tech inventory.
- Let motion support comprehension, never compete with content.
- Use visual restraint as confidence, not emptiness.
- Keep proof close to claims so trust compounds while scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-usable navigation, semantic structure, reduced-motion alternatives, and content that remains visible without animation.
